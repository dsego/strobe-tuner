

A fixed window size in samples produces a constant frequency resolution in Hz,
not in musical units like cents (which are logarithmic).


So if your bin is offset by 10 Hz:

At 440 Hz, that’s ≈ 39.3 cents
At 4186 Hz, that’s only ≈ 4.2 cents


Low-frequency bins (e.g., 440 Hz) will show broad leakage in cents
High-frequency bins (e.g., 4186 Hz) will show narrow leakage in cents
