- green book smoothing





Aligning your DFT frame so the main tone is roughly a whole number of cycles and a half has two advantages.

    The parameters are read from four values of the DFT instead of two, so inherently more noise resistant.

    The second harmonic will fall near a whole number of cycles and thus not have much leakage.
